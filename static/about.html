About 3D Print Jewelry

Covering additive manufacturing for fine-jewelry additive manufacturing — from concept to production hardware.

OUR STORY

Jewelry at the Frontier of Fabrication

3D Print Jewelry covers the full spectrum of additive manufacturing for fine jewelry — from direct precious-metal printing and DLP lost-wax patterns to parametric gemstone settings and bespoke, one-of-a-kind pieces.

Our editorial team includes jewelers, CAD designers, and materials specialists with real bench experience across gold, platinum, and silver, casting, stone-setting, and design-to-fabrication workflows.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover jewelry AM from the printed prong to the finished commission with equal depth — the piece, the process, and the precious material behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for additive manufacturing in fine jewelry — connecting designers and jewelers to the suppliers reshaping how pieces get made.
